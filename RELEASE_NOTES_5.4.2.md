## What's New in v5.4.2

### Spatial Pinch-to-Scroll Across All Windows & Panels
- **Universal Pinch Scrolling**: Enabled spatial hand gesture pinch-and-drag scrolling for every panel, window, tab box, modal, and log container across Grape OS.
- **Bi-directional Navigation (Vertical & Horizontal)**: Moving hand up/down or left/right while holding a pinch gesture smoothly scrolls the target window (`scrollTop` and `scrollLeft`) without interfering with click taps or window tilt.
- **Auto-Detection**: Automatically detects top-most scrollable windows and boxes under the spatial cursor (`#settings-panel`, `#message-list`, AI Chat Hub, Travel Reports table, Music Player library).
